Why I Am Standing

I'm standing because postgraduate students deserve a direct, dependable voice in how the Faculty of Architecture is run. Having studied and worked across HKU, Aalto University, and ETH Zürich, I've seen how differently — and how well — a Faculty can support its postgraduate community when students are properly represented. I want to bring that same clarity and accountability to our Board, as someone who is reachable, consistent, and genuinely invested in the postgraduate experience here.

What I Hope to Contribute

  • A direct channel to the Board — relaying concerns from full-time and part-time postgraduates and explaining Board decisions back in plain language.
  • Stronger support for research students — better studio/lab access and clearer funding information for PhD and research-track postgraduates.
  • An international benchmark — applying what I've seen at HKU, Aalto, and ETH Zürich to how the Faculty supports its postgraduates.
  • A Faculty that keeps pace — advocating for computational/AI-driven design, sustainable construction, and other emerging methods in postgraduate education.
